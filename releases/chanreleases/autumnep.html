On an interview with Sweety High, Charley was asked, "Is there anything else we should know about you or the album right now?" to which he replied, "Yeah. The album’s old music now, and new stuff is coming soon. I’m trying to curate a nice fall sound for this autumn. It’ll be less all over the place, more one genre for the next project, I think." I have listed this as an EP because he just released Mercurial in July and having another album release in 3-5 months seems unrealistic. The "project" being an EP also seems to be the general consensus among the community.

This release is not yet released. Expected September-November 2026.
