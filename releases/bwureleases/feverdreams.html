Fever Dreams is the second studio album by American alt-pop artist BoyWithUke, released on June 4, 2021, independently through TuneCore. The album features 11 tracks that delve into themes of heartbreak, confusion, longing, and the surreal nature of emotional experiences — often framed through vivid, dreamlike imagery. With a sound rooted in lo-fi pop and alternative, Fever Dreams continues BoyWithUke's DIY approach to music, with the artist writing, producing, and recording the project entirely on his own. The album includes fan-favorite songs such as "Hazel Eyes," "Psycho," and "Two Moons," the latter of which later became a breakout hit.
