Trouvaille is an extended play (EP) by American alt-pop artist BoyWithUke, released independently on March 5, 2021. The EP comprises three tracks: "Falling for You," "Tired of Wanting You," and "Animal Crackers," totaling a runtime of 8 minutes and 32 seconds. Entirely self-written and produced by BoyWithUke (Charley Yang), Trouvaille showcases his signature lo-fi, ukulele-driven sound and introspective songwriting. The songs from this EP were not included in any subsequent albums, making it a unique entry in his discography.
