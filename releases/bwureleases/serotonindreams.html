Serotonin Dreams is the third studio album by American alt-pop artist BoyWithUke, released on May 6, 2022, via Mercury Records/Republic Records. The 14-track album explores themes of anxiety, unrequited love, emotional detachment, and existential reflection, all wrapped in BoyWithUke's signature blend of lo-fi pop, alternative, and electronic influences. Written and produced primarily by BoyWithUke himself, the project showcases his growth as both a songwriter and a storyteller. The album includes breakout singles such as "Toxic," "IDGAF" featuring blackbear, and "Long Drives," each contributing to his rise in the alternative pop scene and his reputation for crafting emotionally resonant, melodically rich tracks.
